# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct 🛡️

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming real estate technology ecosystem, we pledge to ensure the Rent Truth BD developer circle remains a collaborative, empathetic, and respectful space for everyone, regardless of experience level, nationality, or identity.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the developer community and Bangladeshi citizens.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment or offensive verbal attacks.
- Publicly exposing private communication coordinates.
- Unprofessional conduct during code review forums.
